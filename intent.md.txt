# Intent – Hiring Pipeline Stall Alerter

## Problem

Recruitment processes can become delayed when candidates remain in the same hiring stage without updates. This can happen due to human error, missed follow-ups, or simply forgetting to check a candidate's status.

These delays can make the hiring process take longer and cause recruiters to lose track of candidates who need attention.

## Target Users

Recruiters and Talent Acquisition teams responsible for managing candidates throughout the hiring process.

## Goal

The goal of the Stall Alerter is to reduce hiring delays, organize follow-up priorities, and prevent candidates from being overlooked.

It helps recruiters quickly identify which candidates need attention and what action should be taken next.

## Input

The tool reads candidate information from a hiring board stored in Google Sheets, including:

- Candidate name
- Current hiring stage
- Last updated date

## How It Works

1. A weekly trigger starts the workflow.
2. The workflow retrieves candidate records from the hiring board.
3. It calculates how many days have passed since each candidate's last update.
4. Candidates with no updates for more than five days are identified.
5. An AI agent analyzes the stalled candidates, assigns a priority level, explains the reason for follow-up, and recommends the next action.
6. The results are formatted into a structured email report and sent to the recruiter as a reminder.

## Scope and Limitations

The tool is designed to support recruiters, not replace their decisions.

It does not:
- Send messages directly to candidates.
- Change candidates' hiring stages.
- Make hiring decisions or accept/reject candidates.

All follow-up decisions remain with the recruiter.

## Success Criteria

The tool is successful when recruiters can quickly understand which candidates need follow-up, why they need attention, and what action should be taken next.

The expected outcome is better visibility across the hiring pipeline, more organized follow-ups, and fewer delays caused by missed or forgotten updates.
